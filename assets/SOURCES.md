# Visual sources and interpretation

These profile visuals combine existing research imagery, native SVG labels and illustrative workspace backgrounds. The linked projects provide the methods, full figures and research status.

## Main banner

The light and dark banners show a **research vision**: connecting retinal vascular morphology and quantitative ocular biomarkers with multimodal health data.

Current public work spans retinal imaging, lipidomics and clinical data. Genomics, wearable signals, microbiome, proteomics and metabolomics are broader integration directions. The connecting lines indicate research themes, rather than established causal effects or a completed joint dataset.

The vessel overlay and topology are cropped from the [published RetinalInsight demonstration](https://inamullah-colab.github.io/news/2026/11/retinalinsight-contextual-dr-grading/), using the [original screenshot](https://inamullah-colab.github.io/images/retinalinsight-current-full.webp). The source image is 2220 × 5309 pixels. The crops are:

| View | x | y | Width | Height |
| :--- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Retinal photograph with vessel skeleton overlay | 269 | 1548 | 628 | 628 |
| Vessel topology | 914 | 1548 | 628 | 628 |

The screenshot identifies red as arteries, blue as veins, and green as remaining binary vessels. These are AutoMorph-derived segmentation predictions. The displayed crops preserve the source pixels and aspect ratio.

The biomarker labels—vessel calibre, vessel density, tortuosity and fractal dimension—follow the measurement categories described in [Zhou et al., *AutoMorph: Automated Retinal Vascular Morphology Quantification Via a Deep Learning Pipeline*, TVST (2022)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9290317/). The banner presents feature categories without assigning numerical measurements to the example image.

The photographic workspace and laboratory surroundings are **AI-generated decorative illustrations**. They do not depict the author's own laboratory, equipment or research data. The scientific image excerpts and exact text labels are composed separately over these backgrounds; the retinal, Grad-CAM, vessel and source-figure crops retain their original image content and aspect ratio.
